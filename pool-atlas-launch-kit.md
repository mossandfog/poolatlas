### Full Description *(paste into the "description" field)*

Pool Atlas is a curated ranking of the world's 100 most extraordinary hotel pools.

Not "nice" pools. Extraordinary ones — pools carved into volcanic rock, suspended 300m above the Amalfi Coast, floating directly on Lake Como, or positioned in near-perfect silence 33 floors above Tokyo.

**What you get:**
→ 100+ ranked pools across 40+ countries
→ Every listing includes features, setting, and direct booking links
→ Editorial covering pool design, architecture, and travel
→ No sponsored listings. No pay-to-play. Just honest curation.

**Why we built it:**
Hotel pool photos are everywhere. Honest curation isn't. Before every trip I'd spend two hours cross-referencing listicles full of sponsored content and stock photos trying to find the pool actually worth visiting. Pool Atlas is the resource I wanted to exist — so I built it.

**How pools are ranked:**
Each pool is evaluated on view, architectural design, uniqueness of setting, and the overall experience it creates. The difference between a "nice" pool and a truly extraordinary one is almost always about positioning and intention, not size or price.

**The current top 5:**

Every pool links directly to booking. Perfect if you're planning where to stay next.

Pin 2–3 times per day for the first two weeks to build momentum with the algorithm. After that, 1 pin per day is enough. Use a free tool like **Tailwind** or **Later** to schedule pins in advance.

**Pro tip:** Re-pin each image to multiple relevant boards. Pin #1 (Grace Hotel) goes to: World's Best Hotel Pools + Infinity Pools + Hotel Pools in Europe + Hotel Pools in the Mediterranean. One image, four pins, four indexable entries.
